# agent-ask-orama v0.2.1

**Released 2026-10-25. A patch release.** One change since [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md): prompt **v17**, which
follows Lake's Quickstart after the person confirms a model (lake#1204). Private repository and image,
`docker.io/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama:v0.2.1`. Tracking issue:
[#57](https://github.com/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama/issues/57).

It is a patch: no setting, route or turn shape changes for a caller. With an older Lake the turn
behaves as in v16.

This release is the tree of `dev` at `3c9685c` (everything in the table below) with these notes, the
version and the README's release line.

### Prompt v17: following the Quickstart after a confirm (#56)

On lake-dev, after Steve confirmed a clustering model, Ask Orama answered "Done: train_quickstart."
and, asked whether training had finished, told him to "configure the segmentation K range", which a
person cannot do. v17 is v16 plus *Following the Quickstart after a confirm*:

- after a confirm, it says what started and what comes next, never "Done: train_quickstart";
- asked about progress, it reads `quickstart_status` for the page's `quickstart_id` in the same turn;
- after a failure (`quickstart_state: failed`, a failed proposal, or the person saying so), it says
  why in the person's words (`model_problem`) and proposes the corrected `train_quickstart` as a card;
- it never tells the person to "configure" graph hints, a K range, a holdout or any engine setting.

`quickstart_text` reads Lake's new page keys (`quickstart_state`, `model`, `model_problem`,
`quickstart_next`) and gives the training, failed and done states their own direction. The deciding
state is unchanged. `PROMPT_VERSION` is `v17`; v14 to v16 stay in `prompts/`. See
[`docs/quickstart/`](../quickstart/README.md).

## Before it rolls: settings

No new setting. The settings of [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md#before-it-rolls-settings) still apply
(`AGENT_SERVICE_TOKEN` and `LAKE_URL` required, both rendered by the operator).

v17 needs the Lake that sends the Quickstart's state on each turn:
[lake#1213](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/pull/1213), merged into lake's `dev` on 2026-10-25 and
not in a lake release yet (lake v0.7.0 does not have it). Against an older Lake the page carries no
`quickstart_state` and every Quickstart turn is treated as deciding, as in v16.

## Not included

Unchanged from [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md#not-included):

- no conversation memory across restarts (conversations are in memory, bounded);
- no explicit workspace ids on data-plane calls, and no idle backoff on the bus
  ([#12](https://github.com/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama/issues/12));
- no startup failure when `AGENT_SERVICE_TOKEN` is missing, and no per-service or per-launch
  authentication ([#45](https://github.com/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama/issues/45));
- no live eval results for prompts v12 to v17, and no live `methodology_draft` eval.

## Known issues

Security-relevant first. All open at this tag.

1. [#45](https://github.com/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama/issues/45) (LSR-180, low): the service starts
   without a token, and the token is one shared credential, not per service or per launch.
2. Lake-side findings that reach this agent, open in other repos:
   [lake#922](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/922) (LSR-013),
   [lake#985](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/985) (LSR-118),
   [lake#989](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/989) (LSR-183),
   [orama-operator#793](https://github.com/oramasearch/orama-operator/issues/793) (LSR-044).
3. [#12](https://github.com/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama/issues/12): no explicit `workspace_id` on
   data-plane calls and delegations, and no idle backoff on the bus.
4. [#27](https://github.com/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama/issues/27): the draft's lock hashes are read
   from `pypi.org`, not confirmed reachable from the pod, and no live `methodology_draft` eval.
5. [#25](https://github.com/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama/issues/25),
   [#23](https://github.com/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama/issues/23): the live evals have not run on
   v12 to v17, nor the count evals. v17 has not been run against a live Lake carrying lake#1213.
6. [lake#1191](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/1191): outside the Quickstart, Ask Orama
   cannot find "the last-uploaded dataset", because `list_datasets` has no upload time.
7. [#3](https://github.com/oramasearch/agent-ask-orama/issues/3): models are listed by project id
   instead of project name.
